Foodlog Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Overview

Foodlog ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Foodlog mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

2. Information We Collect

Google Drive Access: When you log in with Google, we request permission to access your Google Drive with the "drive.file" scope. This allows us to:

  • Create and manage your personal Foodlog spreadsheet
  • Store your meal entries securely in your own Google Drive
  • Read your Foodlog data only when you explicitly use the app

Gemini AI API Key: You provide your personal Gemini API key to enable AI-powered meal analysis. This key is:

  • Stored only in your secure Cloudflare KV storage
  • Never shared with anyone
  • Used only to send your meal descriptions to Google's Gemini API for analysis

App Configuration: We store your preferences (theme, timezone) in secure per-user storage. No personal information beyond what you choose to provide is stored.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in:

  • Google Drive: Your Foodlog spreadsheet (you own it, you can access it anytime)
  • Cloudflare Workers KV: Your API keys and app settings (encrypted, per-user namespacing)

We do not store, access, or view your meal data. Your spreadsheet is entirely yours.

4. What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties
  • We do not use your data for advertising or marketing
  • We do not store your meal entries on our servers
  • We do not access other files in your Google Drive
  • We do not track you across other websites or apps

5. Third-Party Services

Foodlog integrates with:

  • Google: For authentication and Drive/Sheets API access (see Google's Privacy Policy)
  • Cloudflare: For secure storage and Workers hosting (see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy)

6. Security

We implement security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all communication
  • Cloudflare Workers for serverless, isolated processing
  • JWT-based session tokens with HMAC signing
  • Per-user KV namespacing to isolate data

7. Your Rights

You can at any time:

  • Access your Foodlog spreadsheet directly in Google Drive
  • Delete your API keys from the app (they're only stored locally in your secure KV space)
  • Revoke Foodlog's access to your Google account
  • Export or delete your Foodlog spreadsheet

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above.
