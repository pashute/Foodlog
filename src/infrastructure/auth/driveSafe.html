Drive Safe

Your very own Foodlog app
is yours and only yours.

Google Authentication Scope

The drive.file scope of Google authentication allows Foodlog, running on your device and logged in to your user, to write and access only the sheet that it creates for you on your own drive.

Your Privacy Protection

The app cannot see or access any other files or folders on your Google Drive. So the data stays yours and yours only.

Leak prevention

To prevent data leaks when using Gemini AI to estimate the carbs in your meals, the app talks directly to your own Gemini session without anyone else having access to it.
